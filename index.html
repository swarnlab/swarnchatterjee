01 / ABOUT

Advancing Knowledge
Improving Well-being.

I am the Department Head and Bluerock Professor of Financial Planning in the Department of Financial Planning, Housing and Consumer Economics at the University of Georgia.

My work examines how households make financial decisions, how financial well-being intersects with health, and how effective financial planning can support people across life transitions.

I earned my Ph.D. in Consumer Economics, with a concentration in Personal Financial Planning, from Texas Tech University in 2007.
